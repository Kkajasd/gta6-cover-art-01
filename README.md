Leonida Key Art turns one of your photos into a GTA-style cover poster in
three steps: pick a photo, a layout and a grade — then export the PNG.

* Three layouts — Hero (one big panel), Split (two panels), Strip (hero + three small panels).
* Four colour grades — Sunset, Neon Night, Vice Pink, Noir — each with a
  halftone comic treatment, a big title block and a caption strip.
* Drag to pan and zoom each photo inside its panels; one-click Auto-cover
  picks the best layout and grade for your photo.
* Exports 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080 PNGs, entirely offline.
* The two sample scenes are original procedural art — no real person's
  photo and no GTA artwork is used anywhere in the app.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080/ and make a cover.
